Practical advice before you replace a lock

Not every key problem calls for a new lock. Rekeying may make sense after a move if the hardware is in good condition. A door that binds may need attention to its alignment. For vehicles, knowing whether your key has a chip or remote can change what replacement involves.

What we ask when you call

Tell us your location, the hardware or vehicle involved, and whether you can still secure it. We’ll use those details to discuss service availability and what information is needed for an estimate. Have ID or permission from an owner or property manager ready when requested.

Who we help

Homeowners and renters can review our residential locksmith services. Property managers and business owners can explore commercial locksmith services. Drivers can check automotive locksmith services before calling about a lockout, a missing key, or another vehicle lock issue.
