Vehicle lockout assistance

Share the exact location, vehicle make, model and year, and whether the key is inside the cabin or trunk. Have proof of ownership or authorization ready. If a child, vulnerable person, or pet is trapped in a vehicle and in immediate danger, call 911 rather than waiting for locksmith service.

Lost car key replacement and key fob programming

A replacement may need a cut blade, a programmed transponder, a remote, or a combination. Tell us whether you have a working spare, whether the car uses push-button start, and whether all keys are lost. Ask whether the estimate includes cutting, programming, and the service visit. Confirm availability for your exact vehicle before booking.

Broken car keys and ignition problems

Mention whether the key is stuck, broken, difficult to turn, or turns normally but the vehicle won’t start. These symptoms can have different causes, and a no-start condition is not automatically a lock or key problem. If the key is bent or cracked, avoid forcing it. Keep any fragments for the technician if safely available.

Frequently asked questions

Can you make a key if I lost every copy?

It depends on the vehicle and its security system. Provide the make, model, year, and key type so availability can be confirmed. Have proof of ownership or authorization available.

Does a replacement fob always need programming?

Many modern fobs do. A dead battery, failed remote, and an unrecognized transponder can behave differently, so describe what the vehicle does when you try the key.

What affects the price of a new car key?

Vehicle year, key type, parts, whether a working spare exists, and whether cutting or programming is needed can all affect the estimate.
