Commercial lock repair and replacement

Tell us whether the issue affects a storefront, staff entrance, office, or other door. A door that drags, a latch that doesn’t catch, or a key that turns only intermittently gives the technician a useful starting point. Report whether the door can be secured and whether it is part of an exit route; keep required exits usable and don’t improvise a repair that blocks egress.

Rekeying after a staff change or tenant turnover

If company keys aren’t accounted for, identify which doors those keys operated and who needs access next. Rekeying compatible cylinders can retire old keys while retaining usable hardware. Organize doors by location, priority, and operating hours to help coordinate work without leaving staff or tenants locked out.

Master key planning for offices and multi-door properties

Start with a door list and the roles that need access: for example, managers, staff, cleaners, or tenants. A master key system can simplify access while limiting what an individual key opens. Decide who can authorize copies and who will track issued keys before the system is put into use.

Panic hardware and access control inquiries

If you are planning a change to exit hardware or electronic access, identify the door’s use and the existing hardware. Ask about compatibility and any required approvals before authorizing a change. If a required exit is currently blocked, follow your site’s safety procedures and escalate it promptly.

Frequently asked questions

Can we rekey just one door after a key goes missing?

Often, if that key only operated one compatible cylinder. If it was a master key or worked on multiple doors, review the full set of affected openings first.

What should I prepare for a commercial locksmith visit?

Have an authorized contact, door location, hardware details, and preferred access hours ready. Say if the problem affects a public entrance or required exit.

Is a master key system a good fit for a small office?

It depends on how many doors you have and whether staff need different access levels. A door and role list makes the options easier to compare.
